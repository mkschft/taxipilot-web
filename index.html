The Clue Lens method

Do not translate every word. Learn which words matter.

TaxiPilot separates the words that establish the situation, support the correct action and reveal misleading answer options.

Focus words

Understand the main situation, passenger, condition or rule described in the question.

Positive clues

Recognise the short wording that supports the safest, lawful or most professional action.

Negative clues

Spot words such as “only,” “always,” “without” or “not needed” that can expose a trap answer.
